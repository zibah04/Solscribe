SOLSCRIBE 0.3.0
Clear words. Useful notes. Saved on your PC.

GET STARTED
1. Close any older copy of SolScribe.
2. Run SolScribe-Setup-0.3.0.exe from the Output folder in this download.
   Or extract the portable ZIP and open SolScribe.exe inside its folder.
   The installed and portable apps do not need a separate Python installation.
3. Choose your microphone at the bottom right, then click Record meeting.
   Keep "Show words while I speak" checked to see your words during recording.
4. Wait for "Listening" on first use. A speech download may be needed.
   Words appear after a short pause; processing speed depends on your PC.
5. Click Stop recording when you finish. Your recording stays saved.
6. Review the transcript. Double-click any words or speaker names to edit them.
   Improve transcript can reread the complete recording for better accuracy.
7. Click Create notes. Edit your summary, minutes, memo or action list.
8. Choose Save a copy for Word, PDF or text.

ALREADY HAVE A RECORDING OR TEXT?
Use Add a recording for audio/video files, then Create transcript.
Use Paste text for existing notes. Example meeting opens saved sample text;
it does not listen to your microphone.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose Light or Dark under Appearance at the bottom left.
Settings contains language, speech quality and note-style preferences.
Quick notes work immediately. Polished notes need an optional writing helper;
if it is unavailable, SolScribe still makes quick notes and tells you.
Names and setup commands are tucked under Advanced writing setup.

YOUR WORK
Changes save automatically. More contains Rename and Delete meeting.
Open saved files shows where this PC keeps the database and recordings.
Imported recordings stay in their original location; keep those files.
Removing the application does not delete your saved meeting data.
Protect this Windows account and keep backups of important recordings.

BEFORE SHARING A RELEASE
Read RELEASE_NOTES.md for what was tested and the remaining device checks.
This installer is unsigned; a verified publisher requires code signing.
Review recognized words and notes before relying on or sharing them.

BUILDING FROM SOURCE
Developers can use run_windows.bat with Python 3.11 (64-bit), or
build_windows.bat to create an executable. See README.md for developer setup.
