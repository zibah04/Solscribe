SOLSCRIBE 0.4.1 BETA
Clear words. Useful notes. Saved on your PC.

GET STARTED
1. Close any older SolScribe window. Back up existing work before upgrading.
2. Run SolScribe-Setup-0.4.1.exe, or extract the portable ZIP and open
   SolScribe.exe inside its whole folder. No separate Python is needed.
3. The first-use guide helps with appearance, inputs and basic usage.
   You can skip it or reopen it from Guide in the footer or Settings.
4. Choose Microphone, Computer audio or Both sources at the bottom.
   Choose your microphone there; the playback output is in Settings.
5. Click Record meeting. Keep "Show words while I speak" checked for captions.
   First use may download a speech model. Wait for Listening.
   Words appear in short batches plus processing time, not word by word.
6. Click Stop recording. Wait for saving and final captions to finish.
7. Review words and speaker names. Improve transcript lets you compare a
   new draft before replacing your edits. Existing notes remain saved.
8. Click Create notes, review and edit the draft, then Save a copy.

ALREADY HAVE A RECORDING OR TEXT?
Add a recording, then Create transcript. Paste text uses existing words.
Example meeting shows sample text; it does not listen to the microphone.

MAKE IT YOURS
Settings contains Light/Dark appearance, language, speech quality,
playback input and optional writing setup. Quick notes work immediately.
Polished writing uses an optional local Ollama installation. If it is
unavailable, quick notes remain available and the app tells you.
On small windows use the compact top navigation and scroll the workspace.
Record/Stop and recording inputs remain available.

YOUR WORK
Changes save automatically. Delete meeting moves it to recoverable trash.
Settings offers trash recovery and full backup/restore. Imported recordings
stay in their original locations and are not copied into backup archives.
Removing/reinstalling the application preserves the local meeting library.
Keep periodic full backups on another drive. Your database, drafts, audio
and backups are unencrypted; protect this Windows account and backup files.

BEFORE RELYING ON THIS BUILD
This is a beta. Review recognized words, names and notes before sharing.
Physical audio devices and 8 GB laptop performance need target-device checks.
Combined recording keeps both original tracks if you need to inspect a mix.
Check SIGNING_STATUS.txt: no certificate is supplied and builds may be unsigned.
Keep Windows security protections enabled. Updates is in the footer and Settings.
It checks your version, downloads a verified installer and lets you choose Install
and close. Meetings are saved and a database backup is made first. Nothing installs
silently. Optional startup checks can be enabled in the guide or Settings.
Users on 0.4.0 need to install this version once to enable in-app updates.
Read RELEASE_NOTES.md and the repository Windows acceptance checklist.

DEVELOPERS
Use Python 3.11 x64 with run_windows.bat or build_windows.bat.
README.md contains setup, architecture and validation instructions.
